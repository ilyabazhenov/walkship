## Name · Имя

**Walkship** — one capital letter; not WalkShip, not WALKSHIP. In Russian: «Уокшип».
Tagline: **Go for a walk. Come back to a PR.** · «Ушёл гулять — вернулся к готовому PR.»

## Logo · Логотип

Every layout comes in four versions: `on-dark` and `on-light` for the matching background, `black` and `white`
single-colour ones for print and engraving. SVG is the master; PNG is for places that don't take SVG.

| Layout | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `walkship-horizontal-*` | site header, README, captions, slides |
| `walkship-stacked-*` | square spots: avatars, covers, splash screens |
| `walkship-mark-*` | when the name is already nearby: favicon, watermark |
| `walkship-wordmark-*` | when the mark is already nearby: app icon + name |

Logos are flat — no bevel or gradients. The metallic look belongs to the app icon only. Keep clear space around the
logo of at least the height of the mark's centre disc; don't recolour, stretch or outline it.

## Colours · Цвета

| | Dark (Signal) | Light (Paper) |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#0B0C0A` | `#F2F1EB` |
| Disc, accent | pistachio `#B6D98C` | olive `#56752F` |
| Voice ring | silver `#A9AEA3` | graphite `#5E6358` |
| Text | `#E9EBE4` | `#1D1F1A` |

Muted and warm — no neon.

## Type · Шрифты

[Golos Text](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Golos+Text) for text and the wordmark (SemiBold, tracking −1.2 %),
[JetBrains Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono) for labels: uppercase, wide tracking. Both SIL OFL.
